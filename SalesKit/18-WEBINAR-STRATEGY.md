## 1. EVENT DESCRIPTION

### Primary Description (for website, event platforms, and partner communications)

Board governance in government institutions and state corporations across Kenya and Africa remains burdened by manual processes — printed board papers, hand-carried approvals, physical notice distribution, and fragmented resolution tracking. The consequences are significant: delayed decisions, lost accountability, compliance gaps, and unsustainable costs in paper, logistics, and administrative time.

This 45-minute webinar examines the practical realities of digitizing board governance — from the preparation and distribution of board papers through meeting management and post-meeting action tracking. We will walk through the complete lifecycle of a board meeting using the E-Board platform, demonstrating how technology can enforce governance standards while reducing administrative overhead. This is not a theoretical discussion; it is a working demonstration of a platform already built for Kenyan and African institutional requirements, including financial year alignment, conflict of interest management, OTP-based verification, and comprehensive audit trails.

Whether you are a Corporation Secretary managing multiple board committees, a CEO seeking visibility into board resolutions, or an ICT leader evaluating digital governance solutions, this session will give you a clear picture of what digital board governance looks like in practice — and what it takes to implement it.

### Slide 2: The Governance Challenge (2:00–7:00)
**Title:** The Reality of Board Governance Today
**Content:**
- Board papers printed, photocopied, bound, and physically delivered
- Meeting notices sent via email with no confirmation of receipt
- Conflict of interest captured on paper forms — or not captured at all
- Resolutions recorded in minutes but tracked in spreadsheets (or not tracked)
- Audit trail exists only in filing cabinets
- Cost: paper, printing, courier services, administrative time, storage

**Speaker Notes:** Let me start with a question — and I suspect many of you will recognise this. How does your institution currently manage board papers? In most organisations we have worked with, the process looks something like this: papers are prepared in Word, emailed to various reviewers, printed in multiple copies, bound, and physically delivered to board members. Meeting notices go out by email. Conflict of interest is either managed on paper or, honestly, not managed consistently at all. After the meeting, resolutions are captured in the minutes, but tracking whether they are actually implemented often falls through the cracks. And if an auditor asks you to show the governance trail — every approval, every version, every notice — how long does it take you to compile that? This is the reality for most government institutions and state corporations. It is not because people are not trying. It is because the tools have not caught up with the requirements.

### Slide 3: What Digital Board Governance Looks Like (7:00–12:00)
**Title:** The 10-Stage Board Meeting Workflow
**Content — show workflow diagram:**
1. Meeting Initiation
2. Internal Notice
3. CEO Approval
4. Officer Assignments
5. Paper Preparation
6. Director Review
7. CEO Review
8. Agenda Building
9. Live Meeting (attendance + conflict of interest with OTP + signature)
10. Minutes

**Speaker Notes:** E-Board replaces this fragmented process with a structured 10-stage workflow. Every board meeting moves through these stages, and at each stage, the platform enforces the appropriate approvals and notifications. Meeting Initiation creates the meeting record. Internal notices go out to the relevant officers. The CEO approves the meeting. Officers are assigned responsibilities. Board papers are prepared, reviewed by directors, then reviewed by the CEO. The agenda is built. The live meeting is conducted with digital attendance, conflict of interest declarations verified by OTP and digital signature, and minutes are captured. After the meeting, resolutions are created, assigned, and tracked. Every action at every stage is recorded in a comprehensive audit trail. Let me show you what this looks like in practice.

### Slide 8–9: Live Meeting Management — LIVE DEMO (23:00–30:00)
**Title:** Live Meeting: Attendance, Conflict of Interest, and Minutes
**What to demonstrate:**
- Opening a live meeting session
- Digital attendance capture
- Conflict of interest declaration process
- OTP verification sent to the board member
- Digital signature capture using signature pad
- PDF/CSV export of the conflict of interest register
- Agenda-driven meeting flow
- Real-time minute capture
- Minutes export to Word document

**Speaker Notes:** This is the part most people find most compelling. When a board meeting begins, the platform captures attendance digitally. Then — and this is critical for governance — each board member is prompted to declare any conflict of interest related to the agenda items. They receive an OTP to their registered contact, enter it to verify their identity, and then provide their digital signature. This creates a legally defensible record. The system maintains a conflict of interest register that can be exported as PDF or CSV at any time. During the meeting, the agenda drives the flow, and minutes are captured in real time. After the meeting, the minutes can be exported to Word format for further editing or distribution.

### Slide 10: Resolutions and Action Tracking (30:00–34:00)
**Title:** From Decisions to Results: Resolution Tracking
**What to demonstrate:**
- Creating a resolution from the meeting
- Assigning the resolution to a responsible party
- Setting due dates
- Status progression: pending → in progress → completed
- Overdue resolution flagging
- Resolution reporting and dashboard view

**Speaker Notes:** A board meeting that produces decisions without follow-through is a wasted meeting. E-Board ensures every resolution is captured, assigned to a specific person, given a due date, and tracked through to completion. The status moves from pending to in progress to completed — and if a resolution passes its due date, it is flagged as overdue. The dashboard gives the CEO and Corporation Secretary immediate visibility into what has been done and what is outstanding. No more chasing people for updates. No more resolutions lost in the minutes.

- Comprehensive audit trail: action type, user, timestamp, IP address, before/after values
- Audit trail PDF export

**Speaker Notes:** Security is not optional for government institutions. E-Board enforces two-factor authentication using OTP at login. Accounts lock after repeated failed attempts. New users must change their password on first login. Every role sees only what their permissions allow — a Board Member sees their papers and meeting materials; a Superadmin sees the full system configuration. And the audit trail captures everything — every login, every document access, every approval, every change — with before-and-after values and IP address tracking. This trail is exportable as a PDF, which means when auditors come calling, the evidence is ready.
