## DEMO OVERVIEW

**Purpose:** Deliver a compelling 15-minute live demonstration of E-Board that follows a real board meeting workflow from institution setup through audit trail, showing prospects exactly how E-Board transforms their governance operations.

**Target Audience:** Corporation Secretaries, CEOs, Board Chairs, ICT Directors, Internal Audit leads at government institutions, state corporations, NGOs, and organisations with formal boards.

**Key Principle:** Every screen you show must solve a problem the prospect has today. Never demonstrate a feature without first naming the pain it eliminates.

**Pre-Demo Checklist:**
- [ ] Demo environment loaded with realistic institutional data (use a fictional state corporation such as "Kenya National Water Authority")
- [ ] Board members populated with realistic profiles, photos, qualifications, and documents
- [ ] At least one complete meeting cycle (from almanac through minutes) pre-loaded
- [ ] Board papers with annexures at various review stages prepared
- [ ] iPad or tablet available to show board portal experience
- [ ] Stable internet connection tested
- [ ] Projector/screen sharing confirmed working
- [ ] PDF exports pre-generated as backup if internet fails

## MINUTE 0:00 – 2:00 | INTRODUCTION AND DISCOVERY

### What the Salesperson Says

> "Thank you for your time today. Before I show you anything, I want to understand your world. E-Board is a digital board governance platform built specifically for Kenyan and African institutions — state corporations, government bodies, NGOs, and any organisation with a formal board. But the value it delivers depends entirely on the challenges you face today."

> "Let me ask you two quick questions before we begin:"

**Discovery Question 1:**
> "Walk me through what happens from the moment a board meeting is scheduled to the moment minutes are signed off. How many people touch that process, and how long does it typically take?"

*[Listen carefully. Note pain points — printing costs, courier delays, version confusion, last-minute paper changes, missing quorum, unsigned minutes. You will reference these specific pains throughout the demo.]*

**Discovery Question 2:**
> "When your board chair or a regulatory body asks for a record of a specific decision made eighteen months ago — how quickly can you retrieve that, and how confident are you that you have the complete trail?"

*[This question surfaces the audit and compliance pain. Most prospects will admit this takes hours or days and involves searching through physical files.]*

### What Screen/Module They Show

No screen yet. The salesperson's laptop shows the E-Board login page in the background but the focus is entirely on the prospect.

### What Problem It Solves

Establishes that E-Board is not a generic software tool — it was purpose-built for the exact governance workflow the prospect manages. Creates psychological investment by having the prospect articulate their own pain.

### Transition Statement

> "Everything I am about to show you directly addresses what you have just described. Let us start from the very beginning — setting up your institution on E-Board."

## DEMO CONTINGENCY PLANS

**If internet fails:**
- Have pre-generated PDF exports ready on a USB drive
- Walk through screenshots of each module
- Focus the conversation on governance pain points and how E-Board addresses them conceptually

**If the prospect wants to see a specific feature not in the demo flow:**
- Navigate to it directly — E-Board's module-based architecture makes this easy
- If the feature is not available, acknowledge it honestly: "That is not currently in the platform, but let me note it as a potential enhancement and show you what we do offer in that area."

**If multiple stakeholders are present with different interests:**
- Corporation Secretary → Focus on meeting workflow, notices, minutes, papers
- CEO → Focus on approval workflows, dashboards, resolution tracking
- ICT Director → Focus on security, audit trail, permissions, email configuration, branding
- Internal Audit → Focus on audit trail, conflict of interest register, PDF exports

**If the prospect asks about features marked as future enhancements:**
