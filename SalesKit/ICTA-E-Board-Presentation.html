Board governance is a critical function in every state corporation and public institution.

The Challenge

Board Governance in Government Institutions Is Still Largely Manual

Most state corporations and public institutions manage board operations through fragmented, manual processes that create governance risks.

✉

Email-Based Distribution

Board papers circulated as email attachments with no version control or access tracking

📄

Physical Board Packs

Printed documents manually assembled, distributed, and often misplaced or leaked

💬

WhatsApp Groups

Board discussions on personal messaging apps with no audit trail or institutional control

📋

Scattered Records

Minutes, resolutions, and action items tracked in spreadsheets or filing cabinets

These challenges affect governance, compliance, and accountability across hundreds of government boards and state corporations in Kenya.

Multi-Stage Paper Review

Live Meeting Operations

Digital attendance register, OTP-verified conflict of interest declarations with e-signatures, real-time agenda management, and structured minutes capture.

Key Capability

Board Paper Management

A structured workflow replaces email attachments with controlled, versioned, reviewable board papers.

📝

Structured Submission

Officers prepare papers with supporting annexures. Multiple file formats supported up to 20MB per document.

🔍

Three-Stage Review

Papers reviewed by Director, then CEO. Each reviewer can approve, reject with comments, or add annotations.

📈

Version History

Every version of a paper is retained. Rejected papers are revised and resubmitted with a complete audit trail.

🔔

Automated Notifications

Email notifications at every status change — submission, review, approval, rejection. 33+ email templates for the complete workflow.

🔒

Controlled Access

Board members see only papers for their committees. Three visibility levels ensure confidential papers reach only authorized recipients.

Key Capability

Meeting Lifecycle Management

  • Almanac Planning — Annual meeting schedule tied to financial year with CEO approval
  • Meeting Initiation — Create meetings from approved almanac with auto-populated details
  • 10-Day Board Notice — Automated notice generation with Kenyan governance compliance
  • Agenda Building — Drag-and-drop agenda items, assign papers to agenda points
  • Attendance Tracking — Digital register with PDF and CSV export
  • Minutes Capture — Per-agenda-item minutes with Word document export
  • Board Resolutions — Record, assign, set due dates, and track through completion

Conflict of Interest Register

Board members declare conflicts with OTP email verification and digital signature capture. Exportable as PDF or CSV for compliance records.

Resolution Tracking

Every resolution is assigned to a responsible officer with a due date. Status tracked: Pending → In Progress → Completed or Overdue.

Security & Compliance

Enterprise-Grade Security for Government

🔒

Two-Factor Authentication

OTP-based 2FA via email on every login. 6-digit codes with 5-minute expiry, throttled to prevent brute force.

👤

Role-Based Access Control

10 system roles with granular permissions. Each user sees only what their role allows — from CEO to Board Member.

🔐

Account Protection

Automatic lockout after failed login attempts. Forced password change on first login. User status enforcement.

📑

Comprehensive Audit Trail

Every action logged — who, what, when, from where. Before-and-after values captured. IP address and browser recorded. Exportable as PDF.

📝

Document Access Control

Three visibility levels: all board members, committee-only, or specific named individuals. Download tracking on every document.

✅

CSRF & Encryption

Cross-site request forgery protection, encrypted cookies, secure session management, API token auth via Laravel Sanctum.

Implementation

Implementation Timeline

A structured 4-phase engagement ensuring thorough setup, knowledge transfer, and successful go-live.

Phase 1: Platform Setup, Customization & Configuration WEEK 1–2 (14 DAYS)

Server provisioning, E-Board installation, full institutional branding (logos, colors, letterhead), role configuration, workflow customization, SMTP email setup, organizational structure definition, committee setup, and system testing.

Phase 2: Data Migration 3–5 DAYS

Onboard all board members with profiles, photos, and appointment documents. Create financial year, populate almanac with meeting schedule, upload existing institutional documents to the repository.

Phase 3: Training 1 WEEK (5 DAYS)

Hands-on role-specific training for Corporation Secretary, CEO, Directors, Division Heads, Officers, and Board Members. Each role trained on their specific workflows and portal access.

Phase 4: Go-Live & Handover POST-TRAINING

First board meeting managed end-to-end through E-Board with on-site ICTA support. System handover, documentation, and transition to ongoing support.

ICTA Support Team

Two (2) Principal ICT Officers from ICT Authority will be facilitated on-site for the entire implementation period to provide:

  • Platform installation and configuration
  • Institutional customization and branding
  • Data migration and system setup
  • Hands-on training for all user roles
  • Go-live support during first board meeting
  • Knowledge transfer and documentation

Hosting

If your institution has available server space, E-Board can be hosted on your own infrastructure using the recommended minimum specifications:

  • PHP 8.0+ with required extensions
  • MySQL 5.7+ or MariaDB 10.3+
  • Apache 2.4+ or Nginx
  • SSL certificate (required)
  • SMTP email service
  • Minimum 2GB RAM, 20GB storage
