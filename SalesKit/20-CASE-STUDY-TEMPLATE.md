## METRICS COLLECTION FRAMEWORK

### Metrics to Collect from Customers for Stronger Case Studies

The following metrics should be collected at two points: (1) before E-Board implementation (baseline) and (2) after at least 3 board meeting cycles on the platform.

#### Category 1: Time Savings

| Metric | How to Measure | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Board paper preparation time** | Time from first draft to final approved version (days) | Demonstrates workflow efficiency |
| **Board paper distribution time** | Time from CEO approval to delivery to all board members (hours/days) | Shows elimination of physical distribution |
| **Meeting notice preparation and distribution time** | Time from notice creation to delivery to all members (hours) | Demonstrates automation value |
| **Minutes preparation time** | Time from meeting conclusion to draft minutes available (days) | Shows real-time capture benefit |
| **Time to compile governance documents for audit** | Time to assemble requested governance records (hours/days) | Demonstrates audit readiness |
| **Administrative staff time per meeting cycle** | Person-days spent on board operations per meeting cycle | Quantifies labour savings |
| **Time for board members to access papers** | Time from paper availability to board member access (hours/days) | Shows instant digital access benefit |

#### Category 2: Cost Reduction

| Metric | How to Measure | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Printing costs per meeting** | Total printing and photocopying costs per board/committee meeting (KES) | Direct, tangible saving |
| **Paper and binding materials per meeting** | Cost of paper, binding covers, and supplies (KES) | Additional tangible saving |
| **Courier/delivery costs per meeting** | Cost of delivering board packs to members (KES) | Especially significant for members in different locations |
| **Storage costs** | Cost of physical storage for governance documents (KES per year) | Long-term saving |
| **Annual total board operations cost** | Sum of all board-related operational costs (KES) | Headline number for executive summary |

#### Category 3: Compliance and Governance

| Metric | How to Measure | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **10-day notice compliance rate** | Percentage of meetings where notice was sent at least 10 days in advance | Demonstrates regulatory compliance |
| **Conflict of interest declaration rate** | Percentage of board members completing declarations per meeting | Shows governance rigour |
| **Conflict of interest verification rate** | Percentage of declarations verified with OTP | Shows accountability enhancement |
| **Resolution tracking rate** | Percentage of resolutions formally tracked after each meeting | Demonstrates follow-through |
| **Resolution completion rate** | Percentage of resolutions completed by due date | Shows institutional effectiveness |
| **Overdue resolution rate** | Percentage of resolutions past due date | Identifies accountability gaps (before vs. after) |
| **Audit trail completeness** | Whether every governance action is logged with user, timestamp, and details | Demonstrates audit readiness |

#### Category 4: Adoption and Usage

| Metric | How to Measure | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Board member adoption rate** | Percentage of board members actively using the platform | Shows user acceptance |
| **Paper access rate** | Percentage of board members who access papers digitally before meetings | Demonstrates engagement |
| **Average profile completeness** | Average profile completeness score across board members | Shows data quality |
| **Number of board meetings conducted on platform** | Total meetings managed through E-Board since go-live | Shows sustained adoption |
| **Number of board papers processed** | Total papers created and reviewed through the platform | Shows workflow utilisation |
| **Number of resolutions tracked** | Total resolutions created and monitored | Shows resolution management adoption |

#### Category 5: Qualitative Feedback

| Question | Ask Whom | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| "How has E-Board changed your daily governance workflow?" | Corporation Secretary | Personal efficiency story |
| "What is the most significant difference in how the board operates?" | CEO or Chairperson | Strategic governance improvement |
| "How do you find the experience of accessing board papers digitally?" | Board Members | User experience validation |
| "How has E-Board affected your ability to respond to audit queries?" | Internal Audit / Corporation Secretary | Compliance story |
| "What did you spend your time on before that you no longer need to?" | Administrative staff | Time reallocation story |
| "Would you recommend E-Board to a peer institution? Why?" | Any stakeholder | Referral potential and testimonial |

### Data Collection Approach

1. **Baseline survey:** Before implementation, ask the Corporation Secretary and administrative staff to document current metrics (time, cost, compliance rates)
2. **Post-implementation survey:** After 3 full meeting cycles on E-Board, collect the same metrics
3. **Qualitative interviews:** 15-20 minute conversations with 3-4 stakeholders (Corporation Secretary, CEO, one Board Member, Internal Audit)
4. **Platform data:** Extract usage statistics from E-Board (papers processed, meetings conducted, resolutions tracked, audit trail entries)
5. **Financial records:** Request printing, courier, and storage invoices for before/after comparison

### Case Study Writing Guidelines

- **Use specific numbers wherever possible** — "reduced from 5 days to same-day" is stronger than "significantly reduced"
- **Let the customer's words tell the story** — direct quotes are more credible than your description
- **Focus on outcomes, not features** — "100% conflict of interest compliance with OTP verification" rather than "the platform has OTP"
- **Include the before picture** — readers need to understand the starting point to appreciate the improvement
- **Keep it to 2-3 pages** — decision-makers do not read long case studies
- **Use institutional language** — match the formality expected in government and institutional contexts
- **Get written approval** — always obtain written permission from the organisation before publishing
