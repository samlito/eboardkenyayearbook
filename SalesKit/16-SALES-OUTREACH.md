## HOW TO USE THESE TEMPLATES

- **[READY TO USE]** templates can be sent with minimal customisation — fill in the bracketed fields and send.
- **Personalise every message.** Replace [INSTITUTION NAME], [PERSON'S NAME], [THEIR TITLE], and [SPECIFIC DETAIL] with accurate, researched information.
- **Never send generic messages.** If you cannot find a specific detail about the institution to reference, research further before sending.
- **Follow up persistently but respectfully.** Government executives receive many messages. It typically takes 3–5 touchpoints to get a response.
- **Tone guidance:** Professional, consultative, confident. You are a peer offering expertise, not a vendor begging for attention.

## MESSAGING PRINCIPLES

1. **Lead with governance, not technology.** Corporation Secretaries care about compliance, efficiency, and risk — not about Laravel or MySQL.
2. **Be specific about their institution.** Generic messages get ignored. Reference their sector, their governance challenges, their regulatory obligations.
3. **Respect their intelligence.** These are senior professionals. Do not over-explain. Do not use marketing hyperbole.
4. **Be patient.** Government decision-making takes time. Persistence with respect earns trust.
5. **Never disparage their current process.** Even if they are using paper and WhatsApp, respect that it has worked for them so far. Position E-Board as an improvement, not a criticism.
6. **Offer value before asking for anything.** Share an article, a governance insight, a compliance tip. Then ask for a conversation.
7. **Always propose a specific next step.** Never end a message with "Let me know." Always suggest a specific action: "Would Tuesday or Wednesday work for a 20-minute call?"
