## FUNNEL OVERVIEW

The E-Board sales funnel is designed for the institutional and government procurement cycle in Kenya and Africa. Unlike consumer SaaS funnels, this funnel accounts for multi-stakeholder decision-making, formal procurement processes (PPRA/PPAD compliance), ICT committee reviews, and budget cycle dependencies.

**Average expected cycle length:** 60-180 days (government), 30-90 days (private institutional)

```
PROSPECT → DISCOVERY → DEMO → ICT ASSESSMENT → PILOT → PROPOSAL →
PROCUREMENT → IMPLEMENTATION → TRAINING → SUBSCRIPTION → RENEWAL → CROSS-SELL
```

## STAGE 1: PROSPECT

### Objective
Identify and qualify potential organisations that have formal boards requiring governance digitisation.

### Sales Action
- Research target organisations using government registries (State Corporations Advisory Committee, NGO Coordination Board, Capital Markets Authority listed entities)
- Identify Corporation Secretaries, CEOs, ICT Directors, and Board Chairs via LinkedIn, organisation websites, and Kenya Gazette notices
- Attend governance forums, ICPSK events, IoD-K functions, and public sector ICT conferences
- Monitor media for organisations undergoing board restructuring, governance audits, or digital transformation
- Send personalised introductory emails using approved templates (see Part 12 — Email Templates)
- Engage via LinkedIn with governance-related content

### Required Documents
- Target organisation database/spreadsheet
- Company introduction letter (see Part 4 — Company Profile)
- Introductory email templates (see Part 12)
- LinkedIn profile optimised for governance technology

### Exit Criteria
- Organisation confirmed to have a formal board or committee structure
- At least one relevant contact identified (Corporation Secretary, CEO, ICT Head, or Board Chair)
- Initial outreach sent and acknowledged (email opened, LinkedIn connection accepted, or phone call completed)
- Organisation added to CRM with basic profile data

### CRM Status
**PROSPECT** | Lead Source recorded | Contact details captured

## STAGE 2: DISCOVERY

### Objective
Understand the organisation's current board governance processes, pain points, compliance obligations, and decision-making structure.

### Sales Action
- Schedule and conduct a 30-45 minute discovery call or meeting
- Use the Discovery Call Framework (see Part 11 — Objection Handling & Sales Scripts)
- Map current board processes: How do they prepare agendas? Distribute papers? Record minutes? Track resolutions?
- Identify specific pain points: Paper-based processes, delayed notices, lost documents, audit concerns, quorum challenges
- Determine compliance requirements: Mwongozo compliance, sector-specific regulations, audit findings
- Identify all stakeholders: Board Chair, CEO, Corporation Secretary, ICT Director, CFO, Internal Audit
- Understand budget cycle position and procurement process
- Assess technology readiness: Internet connectivity, device availability, existing systems

### Required Documents
- Discovery questionnaire/checklist
- Company profile and capability statement (Part 4)
- Product overview one-pager (Part 5 — Product Brochure)
- Relevant sector case study or reference (Part 7 — Case Studies)
- NDA (if organisation requires confidentiality at this stage)

### Exit Criteria
- Pain points documented and confirmed by prospect
- Decision-making structure and key stakeholders identified
- Budget availability or budget cycle timing confirmed
- Technology environment understood (connectivity, devices, email systems)
- Clear next step agreed (demo date scheduled)
- Prospect confirms E-Board is relevant to their needs

### CRM Status
**DISCOVERY** | Qualified: Yes/No | Pain points logged | Decision-makers mapped | Demo date set

## STAGE 3: DEMO

### Objective
Demonstrate E-Board's capabilities in the context of the organisation's specific pain points and governance requirements.

### Sales Action
- Prepare a tailored demo environment with the organisation's logo, colours, and sample structure (using E-Board's white-label branding capability)
- Customise the demo to address documented pain points from Discovery
- Walk through the complete meeting lifecycle: Initiation to Minutes
- Highlight key differentiators: 10-stage meeting workflow, conflict of interest with OTP, digital signatures, comprehensive audit trail
- Show role-based access: Demonstrate how Board Members, Corporation Secretary, CEO, and Officers each interact with the system
- If presenting to ICT staff, emphasise: Laravel/PHP stack, MySQL database, self-hosted option, security features (OTP 2FA, audit logging, IP tracking)
- Demonstrate iPad-optimised board portal for director convenience
- Address questions and objections in real-time (see Part 11)
- Propose a pilot as the logical next step

### Required Documents
- Demo environment pre-configured with prospect's branding
- Demo script tailored to prospect's sector (see Part 10 — Demo Script)
- Product brochure (Part 5)
- Feature comparison matrix (Part 6 — Competitive Analysis)
- Sector-specific compliance mapping (Part 8 — Government Compliance)

### Exit Criteria
- All key stakeholders attended or received a recording/summary
- Prospect verbally confirms E-Board addresses their core needs
- Specific questions or concerns documented for follow-up
- Agreement to proceed to ICT/Technical Assessment or Pilot
- Follow-up meeting date confirmed

### CRM Status
**DEMO COMPLETED** | Attendees logged | Feedback recorded | Next step confirmed

## STAGE 4: ICT/TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT

### Objective
Satisfy the organisation's ICT department and technical committee that E-Board meets security, infrastructure, and integration requirements.

### Sales Action
- Provide Technical Specifications document (see Part 15 — Technical Documentation)
- Arrange a technical deep-dive session with ICT team
- Address infrastructure requirements: server specifications, PHP 8+, MySQL, SMTP configuration
- Present security architecture: OTP-based 2FA, role-based access control (10 system roles), audit trail with IP tracking, account lockout, forced password change
- Discuss deployment options: Cloud-hosted (Losvinca-managed) vs. self-hosted (on-premise)
- Provide data handling and privacy documentation
- Address backup, disaster recovery, and business continuity provisions
- If required, provide access to a sandbox/test environment for ICT team evaluation
- Respond to any formal ICT evaluation questionnaire or checklist

### Required Documents
- Technical specifications and architecture document (Part 15)
- Security and data protection documentation
- Infrastructure requirements checklist
- ICT evaluation response template
- Deployment options comparison document
- Service Level Agreement draft (Part 17 — Contracts)

### Exit Criteria
- ICT team confirms technical feasibility
- No unresolvable technical blockers identified
- Deployment model agreed (cloud vs. self-hosted)
- ICT team endorsement or sign-off (formal or informal)
- Security concerns addressed and documented

### CRM Status
**ICT ASSESSMENT** | Technical feasibility: Confirmed/Pending | Deployment model selected | ICT contact assigned

## STAGE 5: PILOT

### Objective
Provide a hands-on trial of E-Board within the organisation's actual governance workflow, generating internal champions and building the case for full adoption.

### Sales Action
- Define pilot scope: Typically one committee or 2-3 board meetings
- Execute the Pilot Agreement (see Part 17 — Contracts)
- Deploy pilot instance with full white-label branding (organisation logos, colours, letterhead)
- Run the 6-step setup wizard with the Corporation Secretary or designated administrator
- Configure: financial year (July-June), board members, committees, roles, email templates
- Conduct focused training for pilot participants (Corporation Secretary, 2-3 Board Members, 1-2 Officers)
- Provide dedicated support during pilot period (WhatsApp group, email, phone)
- Track usage metrics: logins, papers uploaded, meetings conducted, documents signed
- Collect feedback at pilot midpoint and conclusion
- Document pilot outcomes and prepare a Pilot Results Report

### Required Documents
- Pilot agreement (Part 17)
- Pilot deployment checklist
- User training materials (Part 16 — Training)
- Pilot feedback questionnaire
- Pilot results report template

### Exit Criteria
- At least one full meeting cycle completed on E-Board (from initiation through minutes)
- Key users (Corporation Secretary, at least 2 Board Members) actively used the system
- Pilot feedback collected and analysed
- Organisation confirms E-Board meets their governance needs
- Decision to proceed to formal procurement confirmed (verbally or in writing)
- Pilot results report prepared and shared with decision-makers

**PILOT** | Start date | End date | Usage metrics | Feedback score | Conversion likelihood (%)

## STAGE 7: PROCUREMENT

### Objective
Navigate the organisation's procurement process to secure a formal purchase order, contract, or Letter of Award.

### Sales Action
- Understand the specific procurement method: Direct procurement, request for quotation, restricted tendering, open tendering (for government entities under PPRA/PPAD Act)
- For government entities: Monitor PPIP (Public Procurement Information Portal) for any published tenders
- Respond to any formal procurement documentation: RFQ, RFP, EOI
- Attend bid opening or evaluation meetings if required
- Provide additional documentation as requested: Audited accounts, equipment list, staff CVs, past performance records
- If single-source/direct procurement: Assist the organisation in preparing justification memo (unique solution, proprietary technology, continuation of service)
- Maintain regular contact with procurement office without being aggressive
- Be prepared for procurement committee review and potential negotiations
- Track procurement milestones: Evaluation, recommendation, approval, award

### Required Documents
- All proposal documents in procurement-specified format
- Company statutory documents (Certificate of Incorporation, CR12, Tax Compliance, PIN)
- Audited financial statements (if required)
- Bid security/performance bond (if required for large contracts)
- Completed procurement forms and declarations
- Priced bill of quantities or pricing schedule

### Exit Criteria
- Purchase Order, Letter of Award, or signed contract received
- Contract terms agreed and executed
- Payment terms confirmed (e.g., 30% on signing, 70% on go-live; or annual subscription invoicing)
- Implementation start date agreed

**PROCUREMENT** | Procurement method | Expected award date | Contract value (KES) | Status: Under evaluation / Awarded / Signed

## STAGE 8: IMPLEMENTATION

### Objective
Deploy E-Board for the organisation with full configuration, data migration, and integration into their governance workflow.

### Sales Action
- Assign an Implementation Manager (may be the same person in early-stage startup)
- Conduct implementation kick-off meeting with key stakeholders
- Execute the 6-step setup wizard: Organisation profile, branding, financial year, board members, committees, roles
- Configure white-label branding: Upload organisation logo, set colours, configure letterhead, customise login page background
- Set up user accounts for all roles: Superadmin, Admin, Corporation Secretary, CEO, Board Members, Officers
- Configure email system: SMTP settings, activate relevant notification templates (33+ templates available)
- Import existing board member data: Profiles, qualifications, documents
- Set up document repository with appropriate categories and visibility levels
- Configure the almanac for the current financial year
- Conduct User Acceptance Testing (UAT) with the Corporation Secretary and ICT team
- Resolve any issues identified during UAT

### Required Documents
- Implementation project plan and timeline
- Configuration checklist
- Organisation data collection form (member details, committee structures, branding assets)
- UAT test scripts and sign-off form
- Go-live checklist

### Exit Criteria
- All configuration completed and verified
- User accounts created for all designated users
- Email notifications tested and functioning
- UAT completed and sign-off received
- Go-live date confirmed
- Training schedule agreed

### CRM Status
**IMPLEMENTATION** | Kick-off date | Go-live target | Configuration: Complete/In progress | UAT: Passed/Pending

## STAGE 9: TRAINING

### Objective
Ensure all users are competent and confident in using E-Board according to their specific roles.

### Sales Action
- Conduct role-based training sessions (see Part 16 — Training Plan):
  - **Corporation Secretary training** (4-6 hours): Full system administration, meeting management, almanac, board papers, minutes, resolutions, conflict of interest
  - **Board Member training** (2-3 hours): iPad portal, reviewing papers, meeting attendance, digital signatures, conflict of interest declaration
  - **CEO training** (2-3 hours): Approvals (notices, almanac), dashboards, document access
  - **Officer training** (3-4 hours): Paper preparation, document uploads, meeting support
  - **ICT Administrator training** (2-3 hours): System administration, user management, audit trail, email configuration
- Provide user guides and quick-reference cards for each role
- Establish a support channel (WhatsApp group, dedicated email, phone)
- Conduct a follow-up refresher session 2-4 weeks after go-live
- Identify and develop internal champions (typically the Corporation Secretary and one tech-savvy Board Member)

### Required Documents
- Role-based training materials (Part 16)
- User guides per role
- Quick-reference cards
- Training attendance register
- Training feedback forms
- Support contact information sheet

### Exit Criteria
- All designated users trained
- Training attendance recorded
- Users demonstrate ability to perform core tasks independently
- Internal champion(s) identified and confirmed
- Support channel established and tested
- Training feedback collected and reviewed

### CRM Status
**TRAINING** | Sessions completed | Users trained | Champion identified | Support channel: Active

## STAGE 11: RENEWAL

### Objective
Secure subscription renewal and deepen the customer relationship for long-term retention.

### Sales Action
- Begin renewal engagement 90 days before subscription expiry
- Conduct a Customer Success Review: Usage statistics, meetings conducted, users active, papers processed
- Present value delivered: Time saved, compliance improvements, audit trail records generated
- Address any outstanding issues or feature requests
- Discuss upcoming E-Board enhancements relevant to the customer
- Present renewal options: Same tier, upgrade, multi-year discount
- If applicable, propose expansion to additional committees or departments
- Issue renewal proposal and invoice 60 days before expiry
- Follow up at 45 days, 30 days, and 14 days before expiry
- If renewal is at risk: Escalate to senior management, conduct an executive-level retention meeting

### Required Documents
- Customer success review report (usage data, value summary)
- Renewal proposal with pricing options
- Updated contract/SLA (if terms have changed)
- Product roadmap summary (relevant upcoming features)

### Exit Criteria
- Renewal contract signed or renewal invoice paid
- Updated subscription terms confirmed
- Next-year almanac or meeting schedule configured
- Customer health confirmed as Green

## STAGE 12: CROSS-SELL

### Objective
Expand revenue per customer through additional services, modules, and referrals.

### Sales Action
- Identify cross-sell opportunities:
  - **Additional committees or subsidiary boards:** Organisations with multiple entities (e.g., county governments with multiple boards, holding companies with subsidiaries)
  - **Additional user licences:** As board membership changes or governance structures expand
  - **Premium support or dedicated account management**
  - **Implementation services for new financial years:** Almanac setup, new board member onboarding
  - **Training for new board members:** Following board appointments or reconstitution
  - **Custom branding updates:** New logos, rebranding exercises
  - **Document repository expansion:** Migrating historical board documents
- Request referrals to peer organisations: "Which other Corporation Secretaries or CEOs would benefit from E-Board?"
- Invite customers to serve as references for new prospects
- Feature customers in case studies (with permission)
- Invite customers to governance forums and networking events where they can advocate for E-Board

- Cross-sell pricing schedule
- Referral programme details (if established)
- Customer referral request template
- Case study consent form

### Exit Criteria
- Cross-sell opportunity identified and proposed
- Additional revenue generated OR referral provided

## FUNNEL MANAGEMENT RULES

1. **No stage skipping:** Every prospect must pass through each stage sequentially. Shortcuts lead to poorly qualified customers and early churn.

2. **Maximum stage duration:** If a prospect stalls at any stage beyond the expected duration, initiate a re-engagement action or move to "Stalled" status.

3. **Weekly pipeline review:** Review all active opportunities weekly. Identify stalled deals, upcoming actions, and resource requirements.

4. **Document everything:** Every meeting, call, email, and decision must be logged in CRM. In institutional sales, continuity is critical as contacts change roles.

5. **Multi-threaded relationships:** Never rely on a single contact. Maintain relationships with at least two stakeholders per opportunity (e.g., Corporation Secretary AND ICT Director).

6. **Budget cycle awareness:** Know the organisation's budget cycle. For government entities, budget preparation begins in October-January for the July-June fiscal year. Time proposals accordingly.

7. **Procurement compliance:** Never circumvent or shortcut procurement processes. Institutional reputation is everything.

8. **Post-sale is pre-renewal:** Customer success activities begin at Implementation, not at Renewal. The renewal decision is made throughout the subscription period.
