## KPI FRAMEWORK OVERVIEW

This document defines the key performance indicators Losvinca should track to measure, manage, and optimise E-Board sales performance. KPIs are organised into five categories:

1. **Pipeline Activity KPIs** — Measuring sales effort and outreach
2. **Conversion KPIs** — Measuring pipeline progression
3. **Revenue KPIs** — Measuring financial performance
4. **Efficiency KPIs** — Measuring cost and time effectiveness
5. **Retention KPIs** — Measuring customer health and growth

Each KPI includes: definition, calculation method, recommended initial target (Year 1 in the Kenyan market), measurement frequency, and the action to take when the KPI is off-track.

---

## 1. PIPELINE ACTIVITY KPIs

### 1.1 Total Leads Generated

**Definition:** The total number of new organisations identified and added to the prospect database, regardless of qualification status.

**Calculation:** Count of new organisation records added to CRM in the period.

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Monthly: 20-30 new leads
- Quarterly: 60-80 new leads
- Year 1: 200-300 total leads

**Measurement Frequency:** Weekly

**Source Breakdown to Track:**
| Lead Source | Target % of Total |
|---|---|
| Outbound prospecting (research, cold outreach) | 40% |
| LinkedIn engagement | 20% |
| Events and networking | 15% |
| Referrals from existing contacts/customers | 15% |
| Webinars and content marketing | 10% |

**Off-Track Action:** If below target, increase outbound prospecting activity. Review target organisation list for untapped segments. Increase LinkedIn posting frequency. Attend more events.

---

### 1.2 Qualified Prospects

**Definition:** Organisations that have been confirmed to have a formal board, an identified decision-maker, and an expressed or implied need for board governance digitisation.

**Calculation:** Count of prospects that pass from "Prospect" to "Discovery" stage in CRM.

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Monthly: 8-12 qualified prospects
- Quarterly: 25-35 qualified prospects
- Year 1: 80-120 qualified prospects

**Lead-to-Qualified Conversion Rate Target:** 35-45%

**Measurement Frequency:** Weekly

**Qualification Criteria (all must be met):**
- Organisation has a formal board or governance committee
- At least one decision-maker or influencer identified by name
- Contact has been made and acknowledged (email response, call, meeting)
- No immediate disqualifying factor (e.g., already using competitor, no budget authority, organisation too small)

**Off-Track Action:** If qualification rate is low, review targeting criteria. Are you reaching the right organisations? If high lead volume but low qualification, tighten lead generation criteria rather than generating more unqualified leads.

---

### 1.3 Meetings Conducted

**Definition:** The number of discovery calls, demos, and follow-up meetings held with qualified prospects.

**Calculation:** Count of logged meetings in CRM.

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Weekly: 3-5 meetings
- Monthly: 12-18 meetings
- Quarterly: 35-50 meetings

**Breakdown:**
| Meeting Type | Monthly Target |
|---|---|
| Discovery meetings | 5-8 |
| Product demonstrations | 3-5 |
| Follow-up/proposal meetings | 3-5 |
| Technical/ICT assessment meetings | 1-2 |

**Measurement Frequency:** Weekly

**Off-Track Action:** If below target, increase phone and email follow-up intensity. Offer more flexible meeting options (virtual vs. in-person). Ensure meeting requests are going to the right contacts (Corporation Secretaries and CEOs, not junior staff who cannot schedule).

---

### 1.4 Demos Delivered

**Definition:** The number of formal E-Board product demonstrations delivered to qualified prospects.

**Calculation:** Count of completed demo sessions.

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Monthly: 3-5 demos
- Quarterly: 10-15 demos
- Year 1: 40-50 demos

**Measurement Frequency:** Weekly

**Off-Track Action:** If below target, review Discovery-to-Demo conversion. Are discovery meetings ending with clear next steps? Is demo scheduling being delayed? Consider offering group demos or webinar-style demos to increase throughput.

---

### 1.5 LinkedIn Activity Metrics

**Definition:** Engagement metrics from LinkedIn content and outreach.

**Metrics to Track:**
| Metric | Monthly Target |
|---|---|
| Posts published | 4-8 |
| Articles published | 1-2 |
| Connection requests sent | 40-60 |
| Connection requests accepted | 20-30 |
| Post impressions | 2,000-5,000 |
| Profile views | 100-200 |
| Direct messages sent (outreach) | 20-30 |

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly

**Off-Track Action:** Improve content quality and relevance. Post about governance challenges, Mwongozo compliance, and board best practices rather than product features. Engage with other people's content (comments, shares) to increase visibility.

## 2. CONVERSION KPIs

### 2.1 Stage Conversion Rates

**Definition:** The percentage of prospects that advance from one funnel stage to the next.

**Calculation:** (Number of prospects advancing to next stage / Number of prospects in current stage) x 100

**Recommended Initial Targets:**

| Stage Transition | Year 1 Target | Minimum Acceptable |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect to Discovery | 35-45% | 25% |
| Discovery to Demo | 50-60% | 40% |
| Demo to Pilot | 40-50% | 30% |
| Pilot to Proposal | 70-80% | 60% |
| Proposal to Signed Contract | 40-50% | 30% |
| **Overall: Lead to Customer** | **3-5%** | **2%** |

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly (review), Quarterly (trend analysis)

**Off-Track Action by Stage:**
- **Low Prospect-to-Discovery:** Improve initial outreach messaging. Target more relevant organisations.
- **Low Discovery-to-Demo:** Strengthen discovery process. Ensure pain points are clearly identified and E-Board's relevance is established before proposing a demo.
- **Low Demo-to-Pilot:** Improve demo quality and customisation. Ensure demos address specific prospect pain points. Review objection handling.
- **Low Pilot-to-Proposal:** Increase pilot support. Ensure prospects complete a full meeting cycle during pilot. Address issues proactively.
- **Low Proposal-to-Contract:** Review pricing competitiveness. Strengthen ROI arguments. Improve proposal quality. Build stronger relationships with decision-makers.

---

### 2.2 Pilot Conversion Rate

**Definition:** The percentage of pilot engagements that convert to paying subscriptions.

**Calculation:** (Number of pilots converted to paid / Total pilots completed) x 100

**Recommended Initial Target:** 60-70%

**Minimum Acceptable:** 50%

**Measurement Frequency:** Per pilot completion, aggregated quarterly

**Off-Track Action:** If below target, review pilot scope and support. Are pilots too short? Too unsupported? Is the organisation completing a full meeting cycle? Are the right stakeholders involved? Consider extending pilot duration or increasing support intensity rather than starting more pilots.

---

### 2.3 Win Rate

**Definition:** The percentage of all formal proposals that result in signed contracts.

**Calculation:** (Number of signed contracts / Number of formal proposals submitted) x 100

**Recommended Initial Target:** 40-50%

**Minimum Acceptable:** 30%

**Measurement Frequency:** Quarterly

**Off-Track Action:** Analyse lost deals. Common reasons: pricing, feature gaps, procurement delays, competitor selection, budget reallocation, change in management. Address the most common loss reason first.

---

### 2.4 Pipeline Velocity

**Definition:** The speed at which opportunities move through the sales funnel.

**Calculation:** (Number of opportunities x Average deal value x Win rate) / Average sales cycle length (days)

**Recommended Initial Target:** Establish baseline in Q1, improve by 10-15% per quarter.

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly

**Off-Track Action:** Identify and unblock stalled opportunities. Remove dead deals from active pipeline. Focus on deals with highest probability and shortest time-to-close.

## 3. REVENUE KPIs

### 3.1 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

**Definition:** The total predictable monthly revenue from all active subscriptions.

**Calculation:** Sum of all active monthly subscription values. For annual subscriptions, divide annual value by 12.

**Recommended Initial Targets:**

| Timeline | MRR Target (KES) |
|---|---|
| End of Month 3 | 50,000 - 100,000 |
| End of Month 6 | 150,000 - 300,000 |
| End of Month 9 | 300,000 - 500,000 |
| End of Year 1 | 500,000 - 800,000 |

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly

**Off-Track Action:** Accelerate conversion of prospects in late-stage pipeline. Review pricing — are deals being discounted too heavily? Pursue higher-value (larger board/more users) organisations.

---

### 3.2 Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)

**Definition:** The annualised value of all active subscriptions.

**Calculation:** MRR x 12

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- End of Year 1: KES 6,000,000 - 10,000,000

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly (tracked), Quarterly (reported)

---

### 3.3 Average Revenue Per Customer (ARPC)

**Definition:** The average annual subscription value per paying customer.

**Calculation:** Total ARR / Number of active paying customers

**Recommended Initial Target:** KES 600,000 - 1,200,000 per year per customer (varies significantly by organisation size — state corporations on the higher end, smaller NGOs on the lower end)

**Measurement Frequency:** Quarterly

**Off-Track Action:** If ARPC is too low, focus prospecting on larger organisations. If too high, ensure pricing is not a barrier to smaller organisations. Consider tiered pricing.

---

### 3.4 Total Contract Value (TCV)

**Definition:** The total value of all signed contracts including multi-year commitments.

**Calculation:** Sum of all signed contract values (including multi-year terms at full value).

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- End of Year 1: KES 8,000,000 - 15,000,000

**Measurement Frequency:** Quarterly

---

### 3.5 New Business Revenue

**Definition:** Revenue from newly acquired customers (first-time subscriptions).

**Calculation:** Sum of first-year subscription values from customers acquired in the period.

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Quarterly: KES 1,500,000 - 3,000,000
- Year 1: KES 6,000,000 - 10,000,000

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly

---

### 3.6 Expansion Revenue

**Definition:** Additional revenue from existing customers through upsells, additional users, or additional services.

**Calculation:** Revenue from existing customers above their initial subscription value.

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Year 1: 10-15% of total revenue (expansion will be minimal initially but should grow)

**Measurement Frequency:** Quarterly

## 4. EFFICIENCY KPIs

### 4.1 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Definition:** The total cost of acquiring a new paying customer.

**Calculation:** Total sales and marketing costs in period / Number of new customers acquired in period

**Include in costs:** Salaries (sales team portion), travel, events, marketing materials, tools (CRM, email, LinkedIn), demo environment hosting, pilot costs.

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Year 1: KES 100,000 - 250,000 per customer (higher in early stages, declining as processes mature)
- Year 2 target: KES 60,000 - 150,000 per customer

**Measurement Frequency:** Quarterly

**Off-Track Action:** If CAC is too high, focus on higher-converting lead sources. Increase referral-based acquisition (lowest CAC). Improve conversion rates at each stage. Reduce time spent on unqualified prospects.

---

### 4.2 CAC Payback Period

**Definition:** The number of months required to recover the cost of acquiring a customer through their subscription revenue.

**Calculation:** CAC / Monthly subscription revenue per customer

**Recommended Initial Target:** 6-12 months

**Maximum Acceptable:** 18 months

**Measurement Frequency:** Quarterly

**Off-Track Action:** If payback period exceeds 12 months, review either CAC (reduce acquisition costs) or ARPC (increase pricing or upsell).

---

### 4.3 Sales Cycle Length

**Definition:** The average number of days from first contact to signed contract.

**Calculation:** Average of (Contract signed date - First contact date) for all deals closed in period.

**Recommended Initial Targets:**

| Customer Type | Target Sales Cycle |
|---|---|
| Private institution (NGO, private company) | 45-90 days |
| Parastatal / State corporation | 90-180 days |
| Government ministry/department | 120-240 days |
| County government entity | 90-180 days |
| **Blended average** | **90-120 days** |

**Measurement Frequency:** Per deal closed, aggregated quarterly

**Off-Track Action:** If sales cycle is too long, identify where deals stall. Common bottlenecks: waiting for demo scheduling (push harder for dates), ICT assessment delays (provide documentation proactively), procurement process (cannot be shortened, but can be anticipated). Pre-qualify budget availability early.

---

### 4.4 Cost Per Lead (CPL)

**Definition:** The average cost to generate one qualified lead.

**Calculation:** Total lead generation costs / Number of qualified leads generated

**Recommended Initial Target:** KES 2,000 - 5,000 per qualified lead

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly

**Off-Track Action:** Shift spend to lower-cost lead sources. LinkedIn organic content and referrals have the lowest CPL; events and paid advertising have the highest.

---

### 4.5 Activity Efficiency Ratios

**Definition:** Ratios measuring the productivity of sales activities.

| Ratio | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Emails to Response | Outreach emails sent per response received | 5:1 to 8:1 |
| Calls to Meeting | Calls made per meeting scheduled | 4:1 to 6:1 |
| Meetings to Demo | Meetings conducted per demo delivered | 2:1 to 3:1 |
| Demos to Pilot | Demos delivered per pilot initiated | 2:1 to 3:1 |
| Pilots to Customer | Pilots completed per paying customer | 1.5:1 to 2:1 |

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly

---

## 5. RETENTION KPIs

### 5.1 Customer Churn Rate

**Definition:** The percentage of customers who cancel or do not renew their subscription in a given period.

**Calculation:** (Customers lost in period / Customers at start of period) x 100

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Annual churn: Less than 15%
- Monthly churn: Less than 1.5%

**Ideal Target (Year 2+):**
- Annual churn: Less than 10%
- Monthly churn: Less than 1%

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly (tracked), Quarterly (action reviewed)

**Off-Track Action:** If churn exceeds target, conduct exit interviews with churned customers. Common institutional churn reasons: change in management, budget cuts, underutilisation, technical issues. Address root causes: increase customer success touchpoints, improve training, ensure usage during board meetings.

---

### 5.2 Revenue Churn Rate

**Definition:** The percentage of recurring revenue lost due to cancellations and downgrades.

**Calculation:** (MRR lost from churned/downgraded customers in period / MRR at start of period) x 100

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Monthly revenue churn: Less than 2%
- Annual revenue churn: Less than 15%

**Measurement Frequency:** Monthly

**Off-Track Action:** If a single large customer churns, it can spike this metric. Diversify customer base to avoid over-reliance on any single account. For large accounts, assign dedicated account management.

---

### 5.3 Net Revenue Retention (NRR)

**Definition:** The percentage of revenue retained from existing customers, including expansions, upsells, and churn.

**Calculation:** (MRR at start of period + Expansion MRR - Churned MRR) / MRR at start of period x 100

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Year 1: 90-100% (acceptable given early-stage dynamics)
- Year 2+: 100-110% (target net positive — expansions should exceed churn)

**Measurement Frequency:** Quarterly

---

### 5.4 Renewal Rate

**Definition:** The percentage of customers who renew their subscription when it comes due.

**Calculation:** (Number of customers who renewed / Number of customers whose subscription was due for renewal) x 100

**Recommended Initial Target:** 80-85%

**Year 2+ Target:** 85-90%

**Measurement Frequency:** Per renewal event, aggregated quarterly

**Off-Track Action:** Begin renewal engagement 90 days before expiry. Conduct customer success reviews. Demonstrate value delivered (meetings conducted, documents managed, audit trail entries). Address any outstanding issues before renewal.

---

### 5.5 Customer Lifetime Value (CLV/LTV)

**Definition:** The predicted total revenue from a customer over the entire duration of their relationship with Losvinca.

**Calculation:** Average Revenue Per Customer Per Year x Average Customer Lifespan (years)

Alternative: ARPC / Annual Churn Rate

**Recommended Initial Target:** KES 2,000,000 - 5,000,000 (based on 3-5 year average lifespan)

**Measurement Frequency:** Annually (recalculated as data accumulates)

**Key Ratio — LTV:CAC:** Should be at least 3:1. If LTV is KES 3,000,000 and CAC is KES 200,000, the ratio is 15:1 (excellent). Below 3:1 indicates the business model needs adjustment.

---

### 5.6 Referral Rate

**Definition:** The percentage of new customers acquired through referrals from existing customers.

**Calculation:** (Number of new customers from referrals / Total new customers) x 100

**Recommended Initial Target:**
- Year 1: 10-15% of new customers from referrals
- Year 2+: 20-30% of new customers from referrals

**Measurement Frequency:** Quarterly

**Off-Track Action:** If referral rate is low, actively ask for referrals at post-implementation and renewal touchpoints. Create a structured referral programme. Corporation Secretaries and CEOs network extensively within their sectors — leverage this.

---

### 5.7 Net Promoter Score (NPS)

**Definition:** A measure of customer satisfaction and likelihood to recommend E-Board.

**Calculation:** Survey customers with the question: "On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend E-Board to a colleague?" NPS = % Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6)

**Recommended Initial Target:** NPS of 30-50 (good for B2B SaaS)

**Year 2+ Target:** NPS of 50+ (excellent)

**Measurement Frequency:** Semi-annually (survey all active customers)

**Off-Track Action:** Follow up with detractors individually. Address their concerns. Identify systemic issues affecting satisfaction.
