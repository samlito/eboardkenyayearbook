## RISK FACTORS AND MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Longer-than-expected government procurement cycles | High | Start government prospects early. Pursue private institutions in parallel for faster wins. |
| Budget cycle misalignment (FY starts July) | Medium | For government: Time proposals to align with October-January budget preparation. Focus on organisations with existing ICT budgets. |
| Difficulty reaching decision-makers | High | Use multiple channels: LinkedIn, email, phone, events, referrals. Target Corporation Secretaries first (they own the board governance problem). |
| Prospects wanting features E-Board does not have (e.g., video conferencing, voting) | Medium | Position as complementary to existing tools (Zoom for video, E-Board for governance workflow). Document feature requests for product roadmap. |
| Technical concerns from ICT teams | Medium | Prepare thorough technical documentation. Offer self-hosted option for security-conscious organisations. |
| Cash flow pressure before first revenue | High | Minimise costs. Pursue quick-win private institutions while building government pipeline. Consider offering discounted first-year pricing for early adopters. |
