# E-Board for CEOs

### Full Visibility. Clear Accountability. Governance You Can Trust.

As CEO, every board decision passes through your office — notice approvals, board paper reviews, almanac sign-offs, resolution follow-up. Yet without a structured system, you rely on verbal updates, email chains, and manual tracking to know where things stand. E-Board gives you a single, secure platform where every governance action is visible, traceable, and under your direct oversight.

---

## What E-Board Delivers for You

### Executive Oversight at a Glance
Your dashboard surfaces what needs your attention right now: board papers awaiting your review, notices pending your approval, almanac submissions, and upcoming meetings. No more chasing updates from the Corporation Secretary or officers. [CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY]

### Structured Approval Workflows
Board notices require your formal approval before distribution. Board papers pass through officer preparation and director review before reaching you for final review — with sticky note annotations so you can provide specific, documented feedback. Almanac schedules are submitted for your approval with full version history. Every approval you grant is digitally recorded with timestamp and audit trail. [CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY]

### Resolution Follow-Through
Board resolutions are captured, assigned to responsible officers with due dates, and tracked through clear status stages: pending, in progress, completed, overdue. You see at a glance which decisions have been acted upon and which have stalled — without relying on manual progress reports. [CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY]

### Institutional Accountability
Every action in E-Board is logged: who created, edited, reviewed, or approved each document; when they did it; what changed. This comprehensive audit trail is your institution's evidence of due diligence — available for export as PDF at any time for oversight bodies, regulators, or internal review. [CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY]

### Secure Information Governance
Sensitive board papers and governance documents are no longer circulated via unsecured email or printed copies left in offices. E-Board controls exactly who sees what — with role-based access, three levels of document visibility, and download tracking. Board members access materials through a secure, iPad-optimized portal. [CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY]

### Conflict of Interest Transparency
Board members declare conflicts of interest through an OTP-verified, digitally signed process — not paper forms that can be lost or disputed. The complete register is exportable for regulatory compliance and institutional records. [CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY]

## Strategic Benefits

**Governance Compliance**
Meet the requirements of the Mwongozo Code of Governance, Companies Act, and State Corporations Act with structured, documented processes — including 10-day board notice requirements and formal conflict of interest management.

**Risk Reduction**
Eliminate the risks of lost documents, untracked resolutions, unsigned declarations, and undocumented approvals. Every governance action carries a digital record.

**Institutional Continuity**
When leadership transitions occur, the institution's complete governance record — every meeting, every paper, every resolution, every approval — remains intact and accessible. No institutional memory is lost.

**Operational Efficiency**
Replace weeks of manual board pack preparation with a structured digital workflow. Automated notifications eliminate the need for follow-up calls and reminder emails. Officers, directors, and the Corporation Secretary work within a single coordinated system.

**Your Institution's Identity**
E-Board carries your institution's brand throughout — your logo, colours, letterhead, favicon, and CEO signature. It presents as your institution's own governance platform, not a third-party tool.
