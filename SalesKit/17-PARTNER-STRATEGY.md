## 17.1 WHY A PARTNER STRATEGY MATTERS

Losvinca is building a governance technology company. It cannot — and should not — attempt to reach every potential client through direct sales alone. A structured partner programme multiplies reach, adds credibility, and creates a referral pipeline that reduces customer acquisition cost.

**The logic is straightforward:**

- Governance consultants are already in the boardroom. They have relationships with Corporation Secretaries, CEOs, and board chairs. They advise on exactly the processes E-Board digitises.
- ICT consultants are already drafting technology specifications for government institutions. They influence what gets procured.
- Legal firms and corporate secretarial firms serve the same clients E-Board targets, often on a retainer basis.
- If these professionals recommend E-Board, the sale comes with built-in trust.

**Losvinca's partner strategy has three objectives:**

1. Generate qualified referrals that convert to paying clients
2. Extend Losvinca's reach without proportionally growing the sales team
3. Build ecosystem credibility — a product recommended by governance professionals carries authority that no amount of advertising can buy

## 17.2 PARTNER CATEGORIES

### CATEGORY 1: Governance Consultants

**Who they are:** Independent consultants and consultancy firms that advise boards on governance best practices, Mwongozo compliance, board charters, governance frameworks, and governance audits.

**Why they would partner with Losvinca:**

- They frequently recommend that institutions adopt structured governance processes — but until now, there has been no local digital tool to recommend.
- E-Board turns their recommendations into a working system. Instead of leaving a governance audit report that gathers dust, they can recommend (and help implement) E-Board.
- Partnering gives them a technology differentiator over competing consultants who can only recommend process changes on paper.

**What they gain:**

- Referral commission: 10% of first-year subscription revenue for each referred client that converts
- Co-branded case studies (with client permission) demonstrating the governance improvement achieved
- Access to a partner demo environment to show E-Board during their consulting engagements
- Priority co-delivery opportunities on implementation projects
- Listing on Losvinca's website as an accredited governance partner

**What Losvinca provides:**

- Partner training on E-Board features (half-day session)
- Marketing collateral they can co-brand or share
- Demonstration environment branded to their consultancy
- Technical support for their client engagements
- Joint proposal templates for governance improvement projects that include E-Board

**Referral model:**

| Referral Outcome | Commission |
|-----------------|------------|
| Referral converts to paid subscription | 10% of Year 1 subscription |
| Referral converts and partner provides implementation support | 10% commission + implementation services fee (agreed per project) |
| Referral converts via tender (partner influenced specification) | 7.5% of Year 1 subscription |

**Partnership pitch (to a governance consultant):**

> "You already advise boards on governance best practices. E-Board turns your recommendations into a working digital system — meeting workflows, conflict of interest registers, audit trails, the entire governance cycle. When you recommend E-Board as part of your consulting engagement, you give your client a tangible, lasting outcome. And you earn a referral commission for every client that subscribes. We handle the technology. You provide the governance expertise. Together, we deliver something neither of us can deliver alone."

## 17.3 PARTNER PROGRAMME STRUCTURE

### Partner Tiers

| Tier | Requirements | Benefits |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Referral Partner** | Sign partner agreement; complete 2-hour orientation | Referral commissions; partner demo environment; co-marketing eligibility |
| **Accredited Partner** | Referral Partner requirements + complete half-day certification; refer minimum 2 clients/year | Higher commission rates (+2.5%); website listing; joint case studies; priority support; quarterly partner briefing |
| **Strategic Partner** | Accredited Partner requirements + refer minimum 5 clients/year OR co-deliver 3 implementations | All Accredited benefits + exclusive sector assignments; joint go-to-market campaigns; product roadmap input; annual partner summit invitation |

### Commission Payment Terms

- Commissions are paid upon receipt of payment from the referred client (not on contract signing).
- Commission applies to Year 1 subscription only (not implementation fees, training, or subsequent year renewals).
- Commissions are paid monthly, 30 days after client payment is received.
- All commissions are subject to applicable tax (withholding tax where required).
- Partner must have a valid partnership agreement on file to receive commissions.
- Referrals must be registered before the client engages with Losvinca directly. A simple email to [PARTNER EMAIL — REQUIRES LOSVINCA INPUT] registers the referral.

## 17.9 RISKS AND MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Partners promise features E-Board does not have | Provide clear capability documentation; partner agreement requires adherence to approved claims |
| Partners approach clients with aggressive sales tactics that damage Losvinca's reputation | Partner code of conduct; right to terminate for breach |
| Commission disputes | Clear referral registration process with timestamps; dispute resolution clause in agreement |
| Partners refer low-quality leads that consume sales resources | Qualify referrals before investing significant sales effort; provide partners with client qualification criteria |
| Partners become competitors (develop their own product) | Non-compete is difficult to enforce; focus on making partnership more valuable than competition; build deep product moat |
| Government procurement rules restrict referral commissions | Commissions are paid by Losvinca to the partner, not charged to the client. This is a standard sales commission arrangement, not a procurement irregularity. However, ensure full transparency and tax compliance. |
