## The Governance Challenge

Boards of directors in government institutions, state corporations, county governments, and regulated organizations face mounting pressure to deliver transparent, accountable, and well-documented governance. Yet many continue to rely on fragmented manual processes:

- **Paper-based board packs** that consume weeks to compile, print, and distribute
- **Inconsistent meeting records** scattered across emails, shared drives, and filing cabinets
- **No centralized oversight** of board resolutions, action items, and compliance deadlines
- **Security vulnerabilities** — sensitive board papers circulated via unsecured email or printed copies with no access controls
- **Compliance gaps** — difficulty demonstrating adherence to the Mwongozo Code, Companies Act, and State Corporations Act requirements for notice periods, conflict of interest declarations, and audit trails
- **Limited visibility** for CEOs and Corporation Secretaries into pending actions, upcoming deadlines, and board member engagement

The cost of inefficient board governance is not merely administrative. It exposes institutions to regulatory risk, delays strategic decisions, erodes stakeholder confidence, and undermines the very accountability that public institutions are mandated to uphold.

---

## The E-Board Solution

E-Board is a purpose-built digital board governance platform designed from the ground up for the specific requirements of Kenyan and African institutional governance.

Unlike generic document management systems or collaboration tools, E-Board provides a structured, end-to-end workflow that mirrors exactly how boards operate — from annual planning and meeting initiation through board paper preparation, live meetings, minutes capture, and resolution tracking.

**E-Board does not attempt to change how your board works. It digitizes and secures the processes your institution already follows — while adding the visibility, accountability, and audit trail that good governance demands.**

## Key Capabilities

### Annual Planning & Scheduling
- **Almanac Management** — Plan the full annual meeting calendar with CEO approval workflow, PDF generation, versioning, and signed document upload
- **Financial Year Alignment** — Built for the Kenyan fiscal year (July-June) with pre-loaded support for all 47 counties

### Structured Meeting Workflow
- **10-Stage Meeting Process** — From initiation through internal notice creation, CEO approval, officer assignments, board paper preparation with annexures, multi-stage review (Officer, Director, CEO), agenda building, live meeting management, to minutes capture and Word export
- **Board Notices** — Generate and distribute formal board notices with CEO approval, 10-day advance notice compliance, and automated email distribution
- **Attendance & Conflict Declarations** — Digital attendance register with OTP verification and digital signatures for conflict of interest declarations

### Board Paper Management
- **Multi-Stage Review** — Board papers move through Officer preparation, Director review, and CEO review with sticky notes, versioning, annexures, status notifications, and recall capability
- **Secure Distribution** — Papers delivered through the platform with role-based access controls, eliminating unsecured email distribution

### Governance & Compliance
- **Board Resolutions** — Create, assign, track, and monitor resolutions with due dates and status tracking (pending, in progress, completed, overdue)
- **Conflict of Interest Register** — OTP-verified declarations with digital signatures and exportable register (PDF/CSV)
- **Comprehensive Audit Trail** — Automatic logging of every action (insert, update, delete) with old and new values, IP address, user agent, and timestamp; filterable viewer with PDF export

### Security & Access Control
- **10 System Roles** — Superadmin, Admin, CEO, Corporation Secretary, Director, Division Head, DD Internal Audit, DD ICT, Board Member, Officer
- **Granular Permissions** — Fine-grained, role-based access control using industry-standard Spatie permission management
- **OTP-Based Two-Factor Authentication** — 6-digit email verification codes with 5-minute expiry and rate throttling
- **Account Security** — Automatic lockout after failed login attempts, forced password change enforcement, user status controls

### Board Member Management
- Comprehensive member profiles with qualifications, documents, photos, CVs, gazette notices, signature images
- Profile completeness scoring to ensure governance-ready records
- Automatic user account creation upon board member registration

### Document Repository
- Categorized document storage supporting multiple formats (up to 20MB)
- Three visibility levels: all members, committee-only, or specific named members
- Download tracking and full access control

### iPad-Optimized Board Portal
- Dedicated portal for board members optimized for iPad use
- Dashboard, calendar, committee view, notices, board papers with sticky notes, active meeting participation, document repository, and profile management

### Communication & Notifications
- Automated workflow emails across 33+ templates covering the full meeting lifecycle
- In-app notification system with per-user tracking, read/unread status, role-scoped delivery, and sidebar badges
- Configurable SMTP email integration with test email functionality

### Reporting & Export
- PDF generation: almanac, notices, attendance register, conflict of interest register, board papers, audit logs
- CSV exports: attendance records, conflict of interest register
- Word export: meeting minutes
