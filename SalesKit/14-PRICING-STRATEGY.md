## 14.1 PRICING PHILOSOPHY

E-Board is a specialised governance platform engineered for regulated institutions — not a generic collaboration tool. Pricing reflects the depth of compliance automation, the rigour of the multi-stage workflow, and the cost of poor governance if an institution does without.

**Guiding principles:**

1. **Value-based, not cost-based.** A single governance failure — missed quorum, undocumented conflict of interest, lost board paper — can trigger regulatory sanctions worth tens of millions of shillings. E-Board eliminates that risk.
2. **Recurring revenue model.** Subscriptions fund continuous hosting, security, updates, and support. One-time fees cover implementation effort.
3. **Transparent tiers, flexible for government.** Published tiers for private-sector and NGO buyers; "Request a Quote" for government and large institutional buyers (see Section 14.8).
4. **Annual commitment rewarded.** Meaningful discount for annual pre-payment to improve cash-flow predictability.
5. **No race to the bottom.** E-Board competes on capability and compliance, not on price.

## 14.8 GOVERNMENT PRICING STRATEGY: PUBLIC vs. "REQUEST A QUOTE"

### Recommendation: Hybrid Approach

**Publish Starter, Professional, and Enterprise pricing publicly** on the Losvinca website and in sales materials. This:
- Establishes market positioning and signals confidence
- Helps private-sector and NGO buyers self-qualify
- Gives procurement officers a reference point for budgeting
- Reduces time spent on low-value enquiries

**Use "Request a Quote" for the Government/Institutional tier.** This:
- Allows flexibility to meet procurement requirements (government quotations must be institution-specific)
- Enables multi-year pricing negotiations
- Accommodates different hosting and compliance requirements
- Permits bundling of implementation, training, and support into a single procurement line item (which many government accounting systems prefer)
- Protects against competitors undercutting on published prices in tender scenarios

**On the website and in brochures, display:**

> *Government & Institutional Pricing*
> E-Board serves [X] government institutions across Kenya. [REQUIRES LOSVINCA INPUT — actual count]
> For a tailored quotation aligned to your institution's requirements and procurement process, contact our Government Sales Team.
> [Request a Quote] button

**In tender/RFQ responses, quote specific prices** drawn from the Enterprise baseline, adjusted for the institution's scope. Never submit a tender response with "pricing upon request."

## 14.15 PRICE REVIEW AND ESCALATION

- Prices reviewed quarterly by Losvinca management.
- Existing clients on annual contracts: price changes effective on renewal only, with 60-day advance notice.
- Existing clients on monthly contracts: 30-day notice for price changes.
- Government contracts: pricing locked for the contract term (typically 1–3 years).
- Annual price escalation cap: 10% (protects against currency fluctuation while maintaining sustainability).
